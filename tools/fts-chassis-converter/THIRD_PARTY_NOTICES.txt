FH6.FtsChassisConverter third-party notices
============================================

This converter is built from the pinned KFPS headless chassis-converter source at:
https://github.com/heyitshestia/kloudys-forza-painter-suite
commit 6f53ca3c584d78659d06d4b4a39561db67d79345

Its model/carbin/modelbin core vendors ForzaTechStudio source corresponding to:
https://github.com/D3FEKT/ForzaTechStudio
reference commit 4f373c5fb192551ce5249e320dd79b1399b693ca

The FH6 Assistant patch restores the ForzaTechStudio render-position reconstruction order in the headless exporter. It does not claim ownership of the upstream parser/importer code.
